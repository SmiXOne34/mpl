# MealWise Family User Guide

## Introduction

MealWise Family is a collaborative meal planning application designed to help families coordinate their meal choices. This guide will walk you through the features and functionality of the application.

## Getting Started

### Account Creation and Login

1. **Register**: Visit the registration page and create an account by providing your name, email, and password.
2. **Login**: Use your email and password to log in to your account.
3. **Password Recovery**: If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset it.

### Dashboard

After logging in, you'll be taken to the dashboard, which provides an overview of:

- Today's meal selection
- Upcoming meal selections
- Notification center
- Quick access to key features

## Meal Selection

### Voting Window

Meal selections are only allowed during specific time windows:

- **Voting Open**: 4:00 PM to 11:00 AM the next day
- **Voting Closed**: 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM

The current voting status is displayed at the top of the meal selection page.

### Selecting Meals

1. Navigate to the "Select Meals" page from the dashboard.
2. Choose a day of the week.
3. Browse the available meals for that day.
4. Click on a meal to view details.
5. Click "Select" to choose that meal for the selected day.
6. You can change your selection at any time during the voting window.

### Weekly Menu

The weekly menu shows all available meal options for the current week:

1. Navigate to the "Weekly Menu" page from the dashboard.
2. Browse meals by day or view all meals for the week.
3. Click on a meal to view details.
4. Use the filter options to find meals by tags or search terms.

## Meal Details

Each meal includes:

- Name and description
- Image
- Tags (e.g., "vegetarian", "quick", "family favorite")
- Ingredients list
- Preparation steps
- Selection button (during voting window)

## Family Selections

See what meals other family members have selected:

1. Navigate to the "Family Selections" section on the dashboard.
2. View selections by day.
3. See which meal is most popular for each day.

## Selection History

Track your past meal selections:

1. Navigate to the "Selection History" page from the dashboard.
2. View your past selections by date.
3. Filter by time period (week, month, all time).
4. Click on any meal to view its details.

## Statistics and Analytics

Analyze your meal preferences and patterns:

1. Navigate to the "Statistics" page from the selection history page.
2. View your most selected meals.
3. See trends by day of the week.
4. Analyze tag preferences.
5. Export your selection history to CSV for further analysis.

## Notifications

Stay informed about important events:

1. View notifications in the notification center on the dashboard.
2. Receive alerts for:
   - New weekly menu availability
   - Reminders to make selections
   - Popular meal announcements
   - Family member selections
3. Click on a notification to view details.
4. Mark notifications as read individually or all at once.

## Profile Management

Manage your account settings:

1. Navigate to the "Profile" page from the dashboard.
2. Update your name, email, or password.
3. Set notification preferences.
4. View your account information.

## User Roles

The application has different user roles with varying permissions:

- **User**: Basic access to make selections and view family selections.
- **Chooser**: Can make meal selections during the voting window.
- **Admin**: Full access to all features, including meal and menu management.

## Admin Features

If you have admin privileges, you can access additional features:

### Meal Management

1. Navigate to the "Admin" section from the dashboard.
2. Select "Manage Meals" to:
   - Create new meals
   - Edit existing meals
   - Delete meals
   - Upload meal images

### Menu Management

1. Navigate to the "Admin" section from the dashboard.
2. Select "Manage Menu" to:
   - Create weekly menus
   - Add or remove meals from the menu
   - Publish menus for family members to see

### User Management

1. Navigate to the "Admin" section from the dashboard.
2. Select "Manage Users" to:
   - View all users
   - Edit user roles
   - Add new users
   - Disable user accounts

## Tips and Best Practices

- **Make Selections Early**: Select your meals as soon as the voting window opens to influence family decisions.
- **Check Family Selections**: See what others have chosen before making your final decision.
- **Use Filters**: Use tags and search to quickly find meals that match your preferences.
- **Review Statistics**: Check your meal statistics to understand your preferences and try new options.
- **Enable Notifications**: Keep notifications enabled to stay informed about new menus and selection deadlines.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

- **Can't Make Selections**: Ensure you're within the voting window (4:00 PM to 11:00 AM).
- **Missing Meals**: The admin may not have added meals to this week's menu yet.
- **No Notifications**: Check your notification settings in your profile.

### Support

If you encounter any issues not covered in this guide, please contact support at support@mealwise-family.com or use the in-app feedback form.

## Privacy and Data Security

- Your meal selections and preferences are only visible to family members.
- Personal information is never shared with third parties.
- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- You can request a copy or deletion of your data at any time through your profile settings.

## Updates and New Features

The application is regularly updated with new features and improvements. Check the "What's New" section on the dashboard for the latest updates.
